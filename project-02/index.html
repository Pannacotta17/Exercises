Kafka on the shore

Kafka on the Shore by Haruki Murakami tells the parallel stories of a teenage boy running away from his past and an elderly man searching for mysterious answers. A surreal and introspective novel exploring identity, destiny, loneliness, and memory.

Men without Women

Men Without Women by Haruki Murakami is a collection of short stories about men dealing with love, loss, loneliness, and emotional isolation. Through intimate and melancholic narratives, it explores the complexities of human relationships and the pain of being alone.
