TENERINA CAKE

Torta tenerina is a cake typical of the city of Ferrara; its deliciousness has won over the entire country—after all, no one can resist a slice of this fantastic chocolate cake!

Ingredients

  • Dark chocolate 200g
  • Butter 100g
  • Eggs 4
  • Sugar 150g
  • 00Flour 50g

Instructions

  1. Melt the dark chocolate and butter together in a bain-marie or microwave. Stir until smooth and let cool slightly.
  2. Separate the egg yolks from the whites. Mix the yolks with sugar, then add the melted chocolate and flour.
  3. Beat the egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff. Gently fold them into the chocolate mixture.
  4. Pour the batter into a greased and lined 22 cm cake tin. Bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes. Let it cool and sprinkle with powdered sugar.
